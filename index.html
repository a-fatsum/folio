About Me

I’m not just a front-end developer, I’m a recipe developer too, so creativity and iteration show up in both my code and my kitchen.

After an unplanned, necessary transition away from aviation, I redirected the discipline and checklist mindset I honed there into software craft.

My background spans the financial sector, aviation, and freelance development, so I’m used to strict requirements, clear client communication, and shipping dependable work on real timelines.

Outside the keyboard I tinker with pen plotters and CNC builds, and I’m usually flying or repairing RC planes and FPV drones, projects that keep my hands busy and my curiosity sharp.

I care about clarity, practical solutions, and the small details that make people comfortable trusting me with their work.

I build reliable, human-readable solutions that people can trust.
